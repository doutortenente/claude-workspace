Bloco 1 — Leitura e interpretação de texto

A Day at the Office

Good morning! My name is Bianca Ramos. I am an administrative assistant at Silva & Costa Ltd., a small import company in Curitiba. I work from 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., from Monday to Friday.

My day always starts in the same way. I arrive at the office at ten to eight, I turn on the computer and I check the e-mails. At 8:15 I print the schedule for my manager, Mr. Duarte. He usually arrives at half past eight and says: "Good morning, Bianca. How are you today?"

The telephone rings a lot in the morning. When I answer, I say: "Good morning, Silva & Costa, Bianca speaking. How may I help you?" Sometimes the caller wants to talk to a colleague who is not at the desk. Then I say: "I'm sorry, he isn't available at the moment. Would you like to leave a message?"

I do not take care of the money. The finance team does that. But I must organize the files, and I have to confirm every meeting one day before.

Yesterday was a busy day. A client called at 9:40 a.m. and asked about an order. The order did not arrive on time because the supplier changed the delivery date. I checked the system, I sent an e-mail to the supplier and I called her back at 11:00. She was angry, but she thanked me at the end of the call.

I like my job. It can be stressful, but you should always be polite and organized. Tomorrow I will start again at 8 o'clock.
